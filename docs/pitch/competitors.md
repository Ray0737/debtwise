# Competitors, without the sales pitch

Updated 15 October 2026. This is for the team and for answering investor questions. It is deliberately not optimistic. Where I could not verify something, it says so.

The map is `competitor-map.png` (and `.svg`) in this folder. Its two axes:

- **Across:** how many of DebtWise's five features the competitor already covers (debt plan, refinance, tax, life events, Thai). 0 to 5.
- **Up:** how many Thais already use and trust it. 0 to 10. These two scores are our judgement, not measured data.
- **Bubble size:** how much they can hurt us.

DebtWise is plotted at 3.5 on the first axis, not 5. We have all five features on paper, but three are shallow (see the honest table below).

## Where DebtWise is weak, in our own words

1. **Three of our five features are shallow today.** Refinance uses sample offers, tax is an estimate that cannot file, and life events handle one one-off bill and an essentials change.
2. **We have no data rails.** Everything depends on the user uploading or typing. Banks get BOT data sharing first.
3. **We run on a competitor's model.** Our consultant uses Google Gemini, with Groq as backup.
4. **No proven demand.** SAM's 20,267 applicants in a quarter show people ask for debt help, but not that they pay for software. Refinn's conversion is a warning.
5. **Trust and rules.** Money advice needs trust. Our terms and privacy are drafts, and we have no licence or partner.
6. **The best substitute is free.** A chatbot, SAM, the bureau and the tax app are all free or already installed.

## What to do about it

- **Be a front door, not a rival, to SAM and BOT.** Send people who qualify to Debt Clinic and cite it, as the consultant already does. Aim for a referral or pilot with SAM.
- **Make the three shallow features real:** real refinance offers with a source and date, a clear tax scope, and more life events than a tuition bill.
- **Own the "checked numbers" story.** Every figure comes from code with a source line to confirm, which a chatbot does not give.
- **Say it plainly in the pitch:** "A chatbot can do part of this. We do it with verified numbers, Thai rules and a monthly plan, and we send you to the free official help first."
